Online Payment Gateway & Bank Payout Setup

How school subscription payments are redirected directly to your real online bank account.
Fund Redirection Workflow:
🏫
1. School Pays Coordinator pays via GCash, Maya, Card, or Bank
⚡
2. Payment Gateway PayMongo settles funds & verifies transactions
🏦
3. Your Bank Account Direct deposit to Landbank, BDO, BPI, or UnionBank

Step-by-Step Directions to Bind Your Real Account:

  1. Create your Merchant Account on PayMongo:
    Register your organization at dashboard.paymongo.com.
  2. Link your Real Philippine Online Bank Account:
    In your PayMongo Dashboard, go to Settings → Payout Settings → Bank Accounts. Select your bank (Landbank, BDO, BPI, Metrobank, UnionBank) and enter your Account Holder Name and Account Number.
  3. Automatic Scheduled Payouts:
    PayMongo automatically deposits collected school licensing payments directly into your registered online bank account every scheduled payout cycle (via PESONet/InstaPay).
  4. Connect API Keys to Grantee:
    Copy your Secret Key (starts with sk_live_ or sk_test_) and Public Key (starts with pk_live_) from PayMongo Developers → API Keys, and paste them into backend-node/.env.
  5. Direct Institutional Bank Transfer (0% Fee):
    You can also update your direct corporate receiving bank numbers in backend-node/.env (under RECEIVING_BANK_1_ACCOUNT_NUMBER) so schools wishing to wire directly via Landbank advice or check will see your exact corporate numbers!
