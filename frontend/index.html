About Grantee

Grantee is dedicated to simplifying educational assistance management for both institutions and students. Our mission is to bridge the gap between educational opportunities and deserving candidates through a centralized, transparent, and secure platform. We believe that financial constraints should never stand in the way of achieving educational excellence.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my application?

Log in to your student dashboard to see real-time status updates and notifications for all your submitted applications.

What documents are required?

Requirements vary by school and educational assistance category, but typically include academic transcripts, proof of enrollment, and valid ID.

Is there an application fee?

Grantee is 100% free for students. Partner institutions review and process applications directly on the platform.
