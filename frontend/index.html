About Grantee

Grantee is dedicated to simplifying scholarship management for both institutions and students. Our mission is to bridge the gap between educational opportunities and deserving candidates through a centralized, transparent, and secure platform. We believe that financial constraints should never stand in the way of achieving educational excellence.

How It Works

Your path to educational funding in three simple steps.

🔍

1. Search

Browse our extensive database to find scholarships that match your academic profile, school, and career goals.

📄

2. Apply

Complete your profile, upload necessary documents, and submit your application securely through our platform.

📈

3. Track

Monitor your application status in real-time, receive timely updates, and get notified when decisions are made.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my application?

Log in to your dashboard to see real-time status updates for all your submitted applications.

What documents are required?

Requirements vary by school, but typically include transcripts, a personal statement, and ID.

Is there an application fee?

Grantee is free for students. Some institutions may have their own processing fees.
