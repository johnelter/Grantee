Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my application?

Log in to your student dashboard to see real-time status updates and notifications for all your submitted applications.

What documents are required?

Requirements vary by school and educational assistance category, but typically include school grades and General Weighted Average.

Is there an application fee?

Grantee is 100% free for students. Partner institutions keep the platform running through a sustainable monthly or annual institutional licensing model managed through the coordinator's account.
