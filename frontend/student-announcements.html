New Scholarship Available!

📅 May 28, 2026 • By Scholarship Office

Leadership
Excellence
Scholarship

We are excited to announce that the Leadership Excellence Scholarship is now open for applications!

This scholarship aims to support outstanding students who demonstrate leadership potential, academic excellence, and a commitment to making a positive impact in their community.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Currently enrolled full-time student
  • Minimum GWA of 1.75
  • Demonstrated leadership experience
  • Good moral character

Application Period:

May 28, 2026 – June 30, 2026

For more details and to apply, visit the Scholarship page.
